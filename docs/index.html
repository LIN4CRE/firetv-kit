🛡️ Why This Setup Kit Is Different

Engineered for rock-solid stability with zero reliance on background computers:

🛡️

Zero-Middleman Ad-Blocking

Configures native Android Private DNS over TLS (dns.adguard-dns.com:853). No VPN app to stall your Wi-Fi, and your PC can be powered off.

⚡

Trailer Bug Permanently Fixed

Disables ExoPlayer Tunnelled Playback to eliminate hardware decoder deadlocks during P2P buffer hesitations. Trailers are never needed again.

🔍

Dynamic Network Discovery

Zero hardcoded devices. Scans whatever Wi-Fi network you connect to in ~500ms, detects model codes, and presents friendly device names.

💾

Portable USB Flash Toolkit

Run prepare-usb.bat to create a standalone thumb drive. Flash any TV from any PC or plug directly into a Fire TV Cube / OTG cable.

🧹

Amazon Telemetry Debloat

Silently disables aggressive retail demos, shopping overlays, and background telemetry while keeping remotes, Alexa, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth 100% functional.

🚀

Ultra Fast Torrent Profile

Pre-tunes Stremio's embedded engine with higher peer limits and optimized buffer chunk sizes for instant playback starts.
